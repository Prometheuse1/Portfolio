StockFlow in detail

The problem

  • Stock, orders and deliveries needed to live in one system
  • Purchase orders (BC) and delivery notes (BL) follow French business conventions and must match the company's physical forms
  • Staff need to find any product or record fast

What I built

  • Global search that jumps to the exact item and highlights its row
  • Editable stock movements that reverse the old stock effect and apply the new one
  • Sortable tables and status cards that recalculate with the active filters
  • Separate BL and BC data models with printable PDFs

Quality and delivery

  • Fixed layout shift from the alert strip and removed an infinite CSS animation
  • Resolved MongoDB TLS connection errors
  • Prepared deployment on Render with an environment template
  • Flagged database credentials to rotate before making the repository public
